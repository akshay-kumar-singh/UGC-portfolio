Privacy policy for Content Studio

Content Studio is the tool Akshay Kumar Singh uses to publish his own videos and send his own emails. This page says what it can access, where that is kept, and how to take the access away.

Last updated 5 October 2026

What Content Studio is

A personal tool. It runs on Akshay's own computer and only he uses it: to post videos to his own channels (After You Tap and Before You Merge, on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook) and to send emails from his own Gmail. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.

What it can access

When Akshay connects an account, the platform asks him to allow access, and the tool uses it only for the following:

  • YouTube (Google): his channel's name and ID; uploading his videos with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails; reading his own videos' statistics and comments, and replying to those comments.
  • Gmail (Google): sending the emails he writes and approves, from his own address; reading the replies in conversations those emails started, and bounce notices. It never opens any other email and never deletes anything.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): publishing his videos to his own Instagram professional account and Facebook Page; reading their own statistics and comments, and replying to those comments.

Where it is kept

  • Sign-ins are kept in a file on Akshay's computer that only his user account can read. They never go into a database and are never shared.
  • Statistics, comments and email replies are kept in Akshay's private database, only so the tool can show them to him.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Nothing is sold or used for advertising. The one exception is a feature Akshay sees directly: when someone replies to one of his emails, the text of that reply is sent to an AI service (Google Gemini or Mistral AI) to sort it and draft an answer for him to review. It is sent only for that.

Google user data

Content Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Content Studio uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Taking the access away

Disconnect in the tool deletes the stored sign-in straight away. Access can also be removed at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It only remembers your light or dark theme choice, in your own browser.
